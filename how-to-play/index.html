Round flow in plain English

1. Betting window

Place chips on Leaf options and/or bonus fish segments while the timer runs. Speed show pacing means windows feel shorter than on sprawling classics — decide stake sizes before the timer, not mid-panic.

2. Wheel resolve

The 53-segment RNG wheel settles. Leaf hits pay cash-style Leaf outcomes. Fish segments open the matching bonus: Lil’ Blues, Big Oranges, or Huge Reds.

3. Bonus fishing (if triggered)

The host reels in multipliers. Official press notes main-game multiplier boosts can carry into bonuses, feeding paths toward up to 5000×. Watch the UI multipliers; do not invent stack rules from memory.

4. Payout and next round

Wins credit per rules; cash caps may truncate extreme outcomes. Then the next speed round opens — reset your plan, don’t auto-double.

Leaf bets — the default learning layer

For a first real-money hour, many Catalog-aware players weight Leaf because Catalog Leaf RTP (up to 97.10%) sits above the bonus mid-95% band. Learning goal: see how often Leaf hits relative to your chip layout, without chasing Huge Reds every round of the show.

Bet board extras — fish sides and All Bonuses

Once Leaf timing feels familiar, add small side stakes on Lil’ Blues / Big Oranges / Huge Reds. Evolution’s product story caps multiplier potential at 100× / 200× / 500× for those rounds. Catalog reminds you bonus RTP is softer, and Huge Reds can feel near ~94.55% effective when caps bite.

Many live UIs also show an All Bonuses control that covers the fish package in one ticket. Treat it as a convenience stake, not a secret edge: confirm what it pays and costs in Help before assuming it is cheaper than three separate fish cards. Rule of thumb for learners: fish / All Bonuses chips ≤ a fixed % of total round stake (see strategy). Never “all-in Reds” to recover Leaf losses.

Controls you must find on desktop and mobile

  • Chip values — match Catalog min ~0.10 where available; start low in MYR terms.
  • Leaf cash card — often one Leaf control with a clear payout label; match it to Help.
  • Fish cards + All Bonuses — optional entertainment sides; read Help before stacking them.
  • Undo / clear — remove mis-clicks before the window closes.
  • Autoplay / repeat bet — dangerous on a speed show; prefer manual rounds while learning.
  • Volume / chat — mute noise if it pushes tilt; chat is not strategy.
  • History — review past results for curiosity only; past Leaf hits do not predict the next RNG outcome.

Reading the bet board like a checklist

Before every Ice Fishing speed round, run a three-second board scan: (1) Leaf chips present at the size you planned, (2) fish sides only if the fun budget remains, (3) undo available if a mis-tap landed on Huge Reds. Official Evolution press frames this as a speed show — that speed is exactly why a checklist beats improvisation. Catalog Leaf RTP up to 97.10% only helps players who still place Leaf deliberately instead of spraying every colour.

On mobile, the scan includes a fourth check: stream lag. If the host audio stutters, skip the round rather than guessing the timer. Malaysia readers on mobile data should treat lag as a forced fold, not a cue to raise.

After a bonus — reset ritual

When Lil’ Blues, Big Oranges, or Huge Reds finishes, clear residual chips, re-open your Leaf plan, and wait one clean round before adding fish sides again. Carry-in boosts (Official press) can leave the UI looking “hot”; that visual heat is not a strategy signal. Return to bonus rounds if you need the multiplier ceilings restated, and to RTP if a big hit tempted you to rewrite mathematics.
