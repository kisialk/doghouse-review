All-bonus coverage

Some skins offer a combined control that covers more than one fishing bonus at once. Convenience is the pitch. Cost is the reality: you are paying for wider coverage every round those cards stay lit. It does not improve the underlying odds of any single segment.

What bonuses do not tell you

  • How often each bonus segment appears
  • Whether a dry stretch means a hit is owed
  • That a theatrical catch equals a max win in the bank
  • That bonuses are “guaranteed” entertainment value in cash terms — they are still chance outcomes inside the show
