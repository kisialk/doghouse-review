RTP status for this publication

Unknown for each bet type until a primary source is locked. We will not average three blogs into one “true” RTP. Missing RTP is a fair reason to leave a table.

Why other sites look more complete

They fill the spreadsheet. Completeness is not the same as evidence. Our advantage is the label: Official where Evolution states it, Competitor-unverified where third parties invent precision, Unknown where we still have nothing.

Observed limits vs universal limits

One operator-skinned capture showed limits around 0.10–10,000 $. That is Observed for that chrome. It is not Evolution’s universal Malaysia bet card.

Boost markers are not RTP

A 3× or 5× leaf explains how that round can pay. It does not replace a published return figure.

How to verify in the client

  1. Open help / game info on the exact Ice Fishing table you loaded.
  2. Record RTP labels per bet type, currency and any stake notes.
  3. Note date, operator and device.
  4. Prefer the in-client sheet over any website table — including this one.
