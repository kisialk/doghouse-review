Responsible Gambling

Adult safer-play notes before and during Ice Fishing Live sessions.

Ice Fishing Live is chance-based gambling. Fast rounds spend money quickly. This page is for adults (18+).

Before you play

  • Only risk money that will not hurt rent, food or bills
  • Set a time stop outside the game UI
  • Do not borrow to gamble
  • Decide the session pot before the first chip

During play

  • Know the total stake before the betting window closes
  • Do not chase Huge Reds after misses
  • A win is a chance to leave — not a reason to raise
  • Treat third-party RTP sheets as trivia until you verify in-client

Bonus tiles are cost

A Huge Reds chip raises exposure. It does not make the segment more likely.

Tools worth using

Where the operator offers them: deposit limits, loss limits, session reminders, cool-offs, self-exclusion. Find them before you need them. Quality shops do not hide these menus.

If play is causing harm

Stop access, lock funds, and look for local support that fits Malaysia. This site is not a treatment service. Account tools differ by operator — check the one you use.
