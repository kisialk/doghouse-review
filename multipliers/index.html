How boosts show on screen

What remains Unknown

A helicopter catch is still a presentation beat — not proof you are near the game ceiling.

Practical habits

  1. Note which segments look boosted before you treat a round as “special.”
  2. Do not raise stakes because a previous boost looked exciting.
  3. Verify any RTP or boost menu inside the client help — not on a blog spreadsheet.
