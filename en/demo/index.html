Observe-first syllabus (30–40 minutes)

Phase A — identity and Help

Confirm Evolution Ice Fishing. Open Help. Tag Official press facts (53 segments, Blues/Oranges/Reds, 100×/200×/500× potentials, up to 5000× max) versus Catalog RTP lines (Leaf up to 97.10%, bonuses mid-95%).

Phase B — silent rounds

Watch 10–15 rounds. Note betting window length, Leaf frequency feel, and how often fish bonuses appear. Do not invent probabilities from this tiny sample.

Phase C — UI rehearsal

If practice chips exist, place Leaf-only layouts. If not, mentally rehearse chip colours, then move to min-stake real money only with a written MYR cap.

Compare practice goals to real-money goals

Observe mode goals are recognition: Leaf vs fish colours, bonus names, timer feel, Help locations. Real-money goals are behavioural: Leaf-primary chips, written MYR stops, no revenge Huge Reds. Confusing the two is how players “finish researching” by depositing without stops. Official Evolution press facts (53 segments, up to 100× / 200× / 500× bonus potentials, up to 5000× max) belong in both phases; Catalog RTP figures belong in the real-money phase as price-of-entertainment literacy.
