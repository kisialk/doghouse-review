All Bonuses on the bet board

Beside the three colour cards, many Ice Fishing tables show an All Bonuses stake that covers the fish package in one chip action. Use it only after Help clarifies cost and payout versus staking Lil’ Blues, Big Oranges, and Huge Reds separately. All Bonuses is convenience, not a hidden higher-RTP shortcut — Catalog still prices fish entertainment softer than Leaf.

How a bonus triggers on the speed wheel

During the betting window you may stake chips on fish colours as well as Leaf. If the 53-segment wheel lands on the matching bonus segment, the show enters that fishing round. Official press emphasizes speed relative to larger Evolution spectacles — less waiting theatre, still real variance when multipliers climb.

If you never stake the fish colour that hits, you are a spectator for that bonus. Side-bet discipline matters: see strategy.

Lil’ Blues — up to 100× potential

Role in the product

Lil’ Blues is the entry fishing bonus in Official press storytelling: lower multiplier ceiling (up to 100×) than Oranges or Reds. Catalog still places its RTP in the softer mid-95% band versus Leaf’s up to 97.10%. Blues suits players who want bonus participation without always paying for Huge Reds tickets.

What Blues is not

Blues is not a “safe bonus.” Mid-95% Catalog RTP still loses long-run. A short Blues highlight reel on social media is selection bias, not a plan.

Big Oranges — up to 200× potential

Big Oranges sits in the middle of the Official press ladder: up to 200× multiplier potential. It is the compromise fish for players who find Blues too mild and Reds too expensive in chip budget. Catalog RTP remains mid-95% class — confirm exact Help lines when shown.

Carry-in boosts from the main game (Official press) can make Oranges sequences feel larger than the base ceiling alone. Still respect cash caps commonly cited near €500,000 in Catalog sources.

Huge Reds — up to 500× potential and softer effective RTP

Huge Reds is the headline fish: Official press multiplier potential up to 500×. Combined with carry-in boosts, paths feed the marketed up to 5000× max win. Catalog frequently cites Huge Reds RTP near 95.17%, with effective return able to drop toward ~94.55% when cash caps bite high stakes.

That is the EEAT warning Malaysia readers need: the most cinematic bonus is also where caps and softer RTP show up most clearly in Catalog tables.

Main-game boosts carrying into bonuses

Official Evolution press states main-game multiplier boosts can carry into bonuses. Practically: a boost earned before the fish sequence may amplify fishing multipliers under show rules. Do not invent stacking formulas from memory — read the on-screen multiplier trail and Help examples.

This carry-in story is why max win marketing reaches up to 5000× without requiring every multiplier to spawn only inside the bonus.

Bankroll rules unique to fish sides

  1. Pre-assign a fish budget (e.g. 15–25% of session risk).
  2. Never move Leaf loss recovery into Huge Reds.
  3. After a big Reds hit, reset chips down — winners tilt too.
  4. If caps are disclosed in Help, assume they apply before dreaming in 5000×.

Ordering the fish ladder without FOMO

A clean entertainment ladder for many Catalog-aware players: learn Leaf first, add Lil’ Blues micro tickets, then Big Oranges, and only then consider occasional Huge Reds. Official press ceilings climb 100× → 200× → 500×, which psychologically pulls stakes upward; your written fish budget must climb slower than the marketing ladder. Catalog mid-95% bonus RTP is the fee for that theatre.

If a streamer skips Blues and only spams Reds, they are producing highlight clips, not teaching bankroll survival. Your Malaysia session is not a highlight factory. After any Reds hit that feels cinematic, force ten Leaf-primary rounds before another Reds ticket — a behavioural circuit breaker, not a superstition about “cooling the wheel.”

Recording what you actually saw

Optional notebook fields after bonuses: which colour, rough multiplier trail, whether a main-game boost carried in (Official press), and whether a cash-cap note appeared. You are not building a predictor dataset; you are checking that your memory matches Help. Memory lies after adrenaline. FAQ — bonus rounds

What does All Bonuses do?

On many tables it packages the fish bonus sides into one stake. Confirm cost and payout in Help before treating it as cheaper or “smarter” than separate colour tickets.

What are the Official multiplier potentials?

Lil’ Blues up to 100×, Big Oranges up to 200×, Huge Reds up to 500× (Evolution launch, 6 Aug 2025).

Can boosts from the main game enter bonuses?

Yes — Official press states main-game multiplier boosts can carry into bonuses toward up to 5000× max win.

Why does Huge Reds RTP look worse in Catalog?

Catalog cites ~95.17% and notes effective RTP can fall near ~94.55% when cash caps bite high stakes.

Are bonuses the same as slot free spins?

No. They are live fishing bonus sequences on a money-wheel game show.
