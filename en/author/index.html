How it all started

Peter began as a regular player. He noticed some products shower bonuses while others drain a bankroll in minutes. That raised a simple question: why does this happen?

Searching for answers led him to the mathematics of casino games — RNG behaviour, RTP, volatility, and how payouts distribute over long play. The hobby became a systematic review method: Official and Help first, Catalog second, marketing last.

From player to researcher

Over time Peter stopped treating casino products as pure entertainment. He reviews sessions with notes on bet choices, feature frequency, bankroll movement, and how interfaces push emotional decisions — without inventing private lab datasets.
